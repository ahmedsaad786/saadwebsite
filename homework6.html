My Illustrated Eye

This is the eye I created in Adobe Illustrator. I loved the gradient used for the iris, because not only does it look beautiful and compliments the brown chosen for the rest of the eye, but I appreciate the implication the colors blue and purple can provide for a sense of curiosity. The eyebrow supports this, as one side is raised almost like the emoji 🤨. The eye lashes are three jagged points on the eye, which I interpret as a kind of defense and cautious element to a somewhat skeptical piece. There’s a sense of curiosity and intent to question, but there’s also a limit of fear and hesitation clashing together.
